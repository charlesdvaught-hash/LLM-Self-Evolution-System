recipe_name: breadcrumbs_merge

simple_explanation: >
  Breadcrumbs transfers only tiny "important traces"
  from one model into another. It is designed to avoid
  overwhelming small models during merges.

best_for:
  - 1B–4B models
  - edge deployment
  - compact reasoning models

bad_for:
  - giant dense systems

strengths:
  - lightweight
  - stable for small models

risks:
  - under-transfer of capabilities

compatible_with:
  - quantization
  - DARE

conflicts_with:
  - MoE routing
