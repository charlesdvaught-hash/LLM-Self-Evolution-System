recipe_name: dare_merge

simple_explanation: >
  DARE keeps only the most important parameter changes
  and throws away weaker ones. This creates sparse merges
  that preserve specialized abilities better.

best_for:
  - reasoning
  - coding
  - specialist retention

bad_for:
  - multilingual balancing
  - weak tiny models

strengths:
  - low forgetting
  - preserves sharp capabilities

risks:
  - instability if too sparse

compatible_with:
  - TIES
  - Breadcrumbs

conflicts_with:
  - dense averaging
