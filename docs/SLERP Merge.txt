recipe_name: slerp_merge

simple_explanation: >
  SLERP merges models by moving smoothly across a curved mathematical path
  instead of averaging directly. This helps preserve the "direction"
  of learned abilities better than normal averaging.

best_for:
  - instruction models
  - stable generalists
  - alignment preservation

bad_for:
  - sparse experts
  - aggressive reasoning specialization

strengths:
  - smoother transitions
  - less destructive averaging

risks:
  - can flatten specialist skills

compatible_with:
  - layerwise merging
  - alignment repair

conflicts_with:
  - high sparsity methods
