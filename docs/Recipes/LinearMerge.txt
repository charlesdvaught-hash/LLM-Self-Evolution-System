recipe_name: linear_merge

simple_explanation: >
  Linear merge simply averages model weights together.
  Think of it like blending two paints evenly into one color.
  It is the simplest merge method and often works surprisingly well for similar models.

best_for:
  - same-family checkpoints
  - safe baseline merges
  - alignment retention

bad_for:
  - highly specialized experts
  - reasoning preservation
  - sparse systems

strengths:
  - simple
  - fast
  - stable

risks:
  - washes out capabilities
  - averaging destroys specialization

compatible_with:
  - SLERP
  - layerwise weighting

conflicts_with:
  - Frankenmerge
  - sparse delta systems
